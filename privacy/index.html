Your information, explained

Privacy policy

Candy Roll · Effective November 5, 2026

Candy Roll is published by XFX, an independent publisher in Canada. XFX is responsible for the information it handles as described here. For support or privacy requests, contact xperiencefx@gmail.com.

You can play without a Candy Roll account.

Your progress and companion care stay in a local save. When enabled, advertising and purchase providers process additional information. Non-personalized advertising does not mean that no data is processed.

1. Your game and your buddy

The game stores progress, scores and an optional player name, pearls, powers, unlocked companions, outfits, food and water levels, affection, settings, heart balance and recovery times, purchase entitlements, age-group choices and advertising limits on your device. Local advertising records include counts and revenue amounts reported by the ad provider; game code does not send these local records to an XFX analytics server.

Device motion is used to steer the ball. Game code does not upload these sensor readings to XFX. There is currently no configured online leaderboard or XFX account server. Apple or Google device backups may retain app data according to your system settings.

Optional buddy reminders

Buddy reminders are off by default. If you enable them and allow iPhone notifications, the game may schedule one local, non-repeating reminder for a predicted food or water need. Requests are scheduled between 9 am and 8 pm, at least 24 hours apart. Returning to the game or giving care cancels the pending request.

The reminder contains your companion’s name and a care suggestion. It may be visible on your lock screen or paired Apple Watch, depending on your device settings. The game saves your reminder choice, scheduling times, visits and care times locally; XFX does not use a push-notification server for this feature. Turn reminders off using the bell in your buddy’s home, or manage notification permission in iPhone Settings. Playing never requires notifications.

2. Advertising

When available, Google AdMob supplies optional rewarded videos. An eligible adult player may choose a video for the stated reward, such as one heart or a power. After applicable privacy choices have been handled, adult players may also see an interstitial between completed levels, around every three victories, with at least 90 seconds of active-play spacing and frequency caps. A rewarded video resets that three-victory interval. There are no banner ads or advertisements during active gameplay. When no interstitial is available, the next level continues. Watching a rewarded video is never required for basic companion care.

Google Mobile Ads and its User Messaging Platform may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, consent choices, crash reports and performance information. These support delivery, measurement, privacy choices and fraud prevention. See Google’s privacy policy and Google’s SDK data disclosure.

The game requests non-personalized ads, disables Google’s publisher first-party identifier, removes Android advertising-ID permission and does not request iOS ATT/IDFA access. Other technical or app-scoped identifiers may still be processed. Required privacy messages appear before ad requests where applicable. Test versions may use Google demonstration ads; tests do not generate advertising income.

3. Purchases, when available

Optional store purchases use Unity IAP with Apple StoreKit or Google Play Billing, accessed through the parent area. Availability varies by build; purchases are disabled in the current private advertising test build. The game never receives the payment card number entered in a store payment window.

When a purchase flow runs, these providers may process products, prices, receipts, transactions, device and app identifiers, session information, language, operating system and country inferred from IP. They use this information for purchase delivery, validation, restoration, transaction reporting and fraud prevention. Purchase events needed to operate Unity IAP are separate from advertising consent. See Unity IAP privacy information, Unity’s privacy policy, Apple’s privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

4. Younger players and shared devices

The game asks for an age group, not an exact birth date. Its advertising service does not initialize for profiles marked under 18 or with an undisclosed age. On a shared device, choose the age group of the youngest player. Playing does not require viewing an ad. Purchase controls are not a replacement for parental supervision and store-level purchase restrictions.

A parent or guardian can contact XFX about information relating to a child. Please avoid including unnecessary personal details in the first message.

5. Your controls and privacy rights

In the game, open Menu → Settings → Privacy to change the age-group choice, read this policy and, when required by the provider, open Ad choices. Changing or withdrawing consent applies to future processing and does not reverse processing already completed.

Resetting progress preserves permanent purchases, privacy choices and ad limits. To remove the local save entirely, clear app storage on Android or fully delete the app on iOS. System backups and provider-held transactions are separate. XFX cannot remotely retrieve or erase a save held only on your phone.

Depending on the law that applies, you may request access, correction, deletion, a copy of information, or withdrawal of consent, and raise an objection or complaint. Contact XFX using the address below. We may ask for proportionate verification and cannot supply information we do not hold. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or your local privacy authority.

6. Support, hosting and sharing

If you email XFX, your email address, message and any information you choose to include are used to respond and handle the request. Email providers process that correspondence. Information may be disclosed where legally required or necessary to protect rights and security.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection data, including IP addresses and technical logs, to deliver and secure the site. XFX adds no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or contact forms to this website. Read the GitHub privacy statement.

Google, Apple, Unity, GitHub and email providers may process information outside Canada. Their policies describe their processing, safeguards and retention practices.

7. Purpose, retention and security

Information is used to run the game, fulfill requested services, answer support requests, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. Where applicable law requires consent, it is sought for the relevant processing; service delivery and legitimate security or support needs may rely on other applicable grounds.

Local saves remain until removed through the app or device. XFX retains support correspondence only as needed to handle the request, follow up and meet applicable legal requirements, then removes or restricts it. Provider-held records follow each provider’s policies. XFX uses reasonable safeguards for information it handles; no system can guarantee absolute security.
